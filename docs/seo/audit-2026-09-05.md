## Problems found, ranked by impact

1. **P1 comparison correctness:** the calculator could present home/router internet as phone data, count a short validity purchase as a monthly solution, accept 90% coverage, or recommend an insufficient fallback while showing savings. Corrected locally.
2. **P1 ranking inconsistency:** MTN's mixed 5GB anytime + 5GB night bundle was classified as night-only. Its R59.80 per anytime GB was excluded while R79/GB was called best monthly value. Corrected classification without changing prices.
3. **P1 freshness enforcement gap:** South African `isVerifiedBundleSource` checks only a status and nonempty date; old records can retain current-looking prices and Offer markup indefinitely. Calculator now gates freshness, but sitewide badges/schema remain a separate release. Static prerendered output still requires rebuilding to update time-dependent labels.
4. **P1 expansion freshness:** Afri Data Cost health check on 5 September reports 32 stale Nigerian bundles (checked 4 August, due 3 September), nine Ghanaian bundles due today (checked 6 August), and 24 fresh other records. `getPublicBundleViews()` filters country but not age. Tables expose dates but do not clearly distinguish overdue records. Local warnings now qualify overdue tables; operator re-verification remains outstanding. Do not fabricate new check dates.
5. **P2 measurement:** fresh GSC and GA4 exports now provide current baselines through 2 September. Validate lead-event semantics and investigate the GA4 channel/session reconciliation before drawing conversion or revenue conclusions.
6. **P2 trust and mobile utility:** calculator lacked named sliders, associated spend label, source links and meaningful empty-state guidance. Corrected. Small existing 10px secondary text and the long answer-to-tool journey remain usability opportunities.
7. **P2 data model drift:** SA catalogue arithmetic uses decimal MB/GB while the calculator used 1,024; expansion uses 1,024. Calculator now matches SA. Cross-country comparisons must explicitly normalize units, tax, validity and unrestricted data before rankings are shared.
8. **P2 script/performance:** main production JS is approximately 408kB uncompressed and shared header approximately 138kB. Third-party ads execute in production-output local preview; an ad request returned 403. This is not evidence of production monetisation failure. Live and local `/favicon.ico` returned 404. ## African expansion recommendation

Keep **datacost.co.za** intact for South Africa and use **country directories on the existing neutral expansion domain**, e.g. `/ng/`, `/ke/`, `/gh/`. This matches the established separation and avoids risking SA equity. Do not implement a migration.

| Option | SEO, technical and operational implications |
|---|---|
| Country subdomains on neutral domain | Clear separation and independent deployment, but more host/property management and duplicated QA; avoid unless teams or infrastructure genuinely require it. |
| Country subdirectories on neutral domain | Shared components, crawlable country navigation and consolidated operations; recommended for new markets. Must isolate currency, evidence, regulations and query ownership. |
| Separate country domains | Strong country identity and autonomy, but separate authority building, domain administration and release processes. Appropriate to retain established SA domain, costly as default for every new market. |
| Country directories under `.co.za` | Easy shared code but an established country-specific domain is a poor neutral home for NG/KE/GH positioning. |
| Move SA to a neutral-domain `/za/` | Could simplify the eventual platform, but creates an unnecessary migration now. Needs traffic evidence, redirects, ownership mapping and rollback. |

This recommendation is an inference from the existing project and Google's regional URL guidance, not a guaranteed ranking advantage. Use self-canonicals, country-specific sitemaps/measurement, explicit country navigation, no IP-forced redirects, and reciprocal hreflang only for genuinely equivalent pages. Do not cross-canonical distinct country content. [Google regional-site guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/managing-multi-regional-sites).
